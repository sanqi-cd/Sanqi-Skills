# Paper Explanation Quality Rubric

Score each dimension from 0 to 2. Revise any output below 10/12 or with a zero in evidence integrity.

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Evidence integrity | Invents or overstates source content | Boundaries are stated but attribution is inconsistent | Every major claim is attributable and uncertainty is explicit |
| Explanatory clarity | Mostly translation or jargon | Some intuition but important jumps remain | Builds from problem and intuition to mechanism and consequence |
| Method accuracy | Core method is wrong or incomplete | Main flow is right but assumptions are thin | Inputs, transformations, objectives, assumptions, and formulas align |
| Experiment reading | Lists headline numbers only | Covers baselines and some results | Explains setup, metrics, comparisons, ablations, and what they do not prove |
| Reproducibility | Omits implementation details | Records some details | Separates reported values, missing details, and inferred requirements |
| Critical synthesis | Repeats the authors' conclusion | Names generic limitations | Relates evidence to claims, identifies scope, and proposes concrete follow-up tests |

## Attribution labels

- **作者陈述**: a claim explicitly made by the paper.
- **论文证据**: a result shown in a table, figure, equation, appendix, or reported observation.
- **解读**: the explainer's analogy, inference, critique, or synthesis.
- **未知**: information absent, unreadable, or unavailable in the current source.

Attach a locator such as `[p. 7]`, `[§3.2]`, `[Figure 4]`, or `[Table 2]` to major claims. If the source has no stable page numbers, use section, figure, table, or quoted heading locators.
